## 1. Charter Purpose

This Charter formally defines the **mandate, limits, and responsibilities** of:

> **Omnex_System_Foundation**

Omnex_System_Foundation exists to provide a **shared semantic substrate** for the entire Omnex System-of-Systems.

This Charter is **binding**.

---

## 2. Constitutional Position

Omnex_System_Foundation is a **Core Foundation System**, operating **under the supremacy** of Omnex_System_Core.

It is **not** a sovereign authority.

Its function is **semantic**, not executive.

---

## 3. Foundational Assertion


This principle governs all design, implementation, and interpretation of the Foundation system.

---

## 4. Charter Mandate

Omnex_System_Foundation SHALL provide:

- Canonical vocabularies
- Shared classifications
- Enumerated semantic primitives
- Cross-system reference meanings
- Stable interpretive constructs

These definitions MUST be:

- Deterministic
- Immutable once ratified
- Versioned through append-only law
- Universally reusable

---

## 5. Scope of Semantic Authority

Omnex_System_Foundation MAY define semantics for:

- Status values (e.g. ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, REVOKED)
- Severity levels
- Outcome classifications
- Temporal semantics (effective_from, effective_to)
- Canonical labels and codes
- Measurement and unit meaning
- Cross-domain reference terms

All such definitions are **descriptive**, never prescriptive.

---

## 6. Explicit Prohibitions

Omnex_System_Foundation SHALL NOT:

- Register systems
- Activate systems
- Authorize actions
- Enforce outcomes
- Validate legality
- Override governance decisions
- Produce operational side effects

Any attempt to use Foundation semantics as **authority** constitutes a **constitutional breach**.

---

## 7. Dependency Rule

All Omnex systems:

- MAY depend on Omnex_System_Foundation for **meaning**
- MUST depend on Omnex_System_Core for **authority**

Formally:


No inverse dependency is permitted.

---

## 8. Relationship to Other Core Foundation Systems

### 8.1 Omnex_System_Core
- Grants existence and legitimacy
- Ratifies Foundation semantic artefacts

### 8.2 Omnex_System_Identity
- Uses Foundation semantics to describe identity states
- Does not inherit authority from Foundation

### 8.3 Omnex_System_Governance
- Applies law using Foundation-defined language
- Governance outcomes remain sovereign

### 8.4 Omnex_System_Audit
- Records facts using Foundation semantics
- Audit truth is not derived from Foundation meaning

---

## 9. Semantic Stability Rule

Once a semantic construct is ratified:

- It SHALL NOT be mutated
- It SHALL NOT be redefined
- It SHALL NOT be deleted

Change is permitted **only by extension**, via:

- New semantic entries
- Explicit versioning
- Append-only constitutional artefacts

---

## 10. Implementation Expectations

Omnex_System_Foundation SHOULD be implemented as:

- Canonical reference tables
- Enumerations
- Registries
- Controlled vocabularies

It SHALL NOT implement:

- Business logic
- Workflow logic
- Enforcement logic
- Orchestration logic

---

## 11. Enforcement

This Charter SHALL be enforced through:

- Architectural review
- Engine boundary enforcement
- Migration law
- Code review
- Institutional training

Violations constitute a **Core Foundation violation**.

---

## 12. Permanence Clause

This Charter is:

- Evergreen
- Append-only
- Non-deprecated

Amendment requires:

- A new constitutional artefact
- Explicit Omnex_System_Core ratification
- Epoch declaration

---

## 13. Closing Declaration


This Charter ensures that:

- Semantics unify, never dominate
- Systems remain interoperable
- Sovereignty remains intact
- Omnex remains intelligible across generations
